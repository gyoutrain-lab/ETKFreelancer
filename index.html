Why Internal Linking Architecture Drives Successful Website Crawling

Published by ETKFreelancer Engineering Team • 5 Min Read

In modern search engine optimization, internal linking is not merely an aesthetic navigation tool for human visitors, but a vital roadmap for search bots (such as Googlebot). Without a structured link distribution connecting service pages, legal documents, and information sections, search crawlers may struggle to index pages efficiently.

Through clean silo architectures, each page reinforces mutual authority, boosts readability scores, and increases organic click-through rates without relying exclusively on expensive paid ad campaigns.

Privacy Policy Compliance Standards for Analytics and Pixel Integration

Published by ETKFreelancer Engineering Team • 4 Min Read

When embedding third-party tracking scripts such as Google Analytics 4 or Meta Pixel, user transparency becomes an absolute obligation. Visitors must be informed clearly regarding cookie data collection and performance analytics.

Providing a transparent Privacy Policy within the site serves as a crucial parameter evaluated by manual reviewers prior to approving advertising placements.
